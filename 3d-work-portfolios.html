3D Work Portfolios

This thread captures my journey and progress as I dive into the world of 3D CG works.

Disclaimer: Some are personal projects, not for commercial purposes. It was intended for learning and skill development only, and some are commissioned pieces for several brands.
CATEGORY: 3D Art SOFTWARE: Blender, After Effect, Premiere Pro UPDATE: Regularly
01

The Abandoned Cabin

An exploration of environment creation in Blender, featuring an abandoned cabin surrounded by a quiet and overgrown landscape. This project focuses on creating a sense of age, isolation, and atmosphere through 3D modeling, texturing, lighting, and composition.

02

Forza Horizon 6 Trailer (fan made)

Recreating a Forza Horizon 6–inspired trailer using Blender 3D with a realistic visual approach. Set in a Japanese environment, this project focuses on atmosphere, lighting, environment details, and cinematic storytelling.

03

Kawal Putusan MK

A personal 3D project created in response to the public demonstrations surrounding the Constitutional Court’s decision. This render represents my contribution as a 3D artist—using visual storytelling to highlight that every voice, no matter how small, can have an impact. Through this piece, I aimed to capture the atmosphere of collective action and the importance of standing together.

04

Realistic Approach!

On the journey to "learning" how to create animation with a realistic approach through simple scene experiments, it's all about composition, light, and materials. Using tools and methods I've never worked with before, and also diving deeper into my previous workflows. It's been a lot of fun, and I've learned so much from this personal project. Yes it is! Enjoy! Gigantic shout out to @stache_obj for allowing me to recreate and be inspired by his work, go check out his page.

05

FOX 3

just doing some extra exploration. Picking up tricks from MLT Studios on how to create detailed environments and other cool simple techniques, that not draining my VRAM. I’m also learning new methods also updating my previous workflow and patching up a few gaps.

06

Underwater Deep Oceon

Continuing the journey of underwater animation, there have been several upgrades and improvements made, such as water simulation, adding extra details, and more!

07

NR Hair Care

A 3D animation product commission from NR Hair Care. This video showcases the key natural ingredients of the shampoo through an elegant visual sequence, highlighting purity, texture, and premium quality. I crafted each element—modeling, lighting, and animation—to reflect the brand’s clean, luxurious identity and deliver a smooth, refined presentation.

08

GUTE

A 3D animation product commission from GUTE. This project showcases my ability to create detailed, stylized visuals and deliver high-quality animations tailored to the client’s vision. I handled the entire process—from modeling and texturing to animation and final rendering—ensuring a polished and engaging result.

09

FERGUS

A 3D animation product commission from Fergus, an Australian dog-food brand. This April Fools concept imagines the iconic Fergus kibble crashing into the Sydney Opera House in a playful, over-the-top scene. I created the full 3D environment, animation, and render to capture a fun, humorous moment while keeping the brand’s friendly identity intact.

10

Collage Final Project!

Applying the results of my practice to create a final project for my design degree, addressing the issue of the dangers of plastic waste to marine ecosystems, presented through 3D animation.

11

Cussons Baby

A 3D interior design project created for Cussons Baby as custom studio backgrounds. I designed and rendered several nursery-themed rooms to be used as backdrop plates for their photo sessions. The final 3D environments are crafted to seamlessly integrate with their model photography with Digital Imaging Method—allowing the combined images to appear natural, cohesive, and visually complete.
