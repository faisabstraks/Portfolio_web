3D Work Portfolios

This thread captures my journey and progress as I dive into the world of 3D CG works.

Disclaimer: Some are personal projects, not for commercial purposes. It was intended for learning and skill development only, and some are commissioned pieces for several brands.
CATEGORY: 3D Art SOFTWARE: Blender UPDATE: Regularly
01

The Abandoned Cabin

An exploration of environment creation in Blender, featuring an abandoned cabin surrounded by a quiet and overgrown landscape. This project focuses on creating a sense of age, isolation, and atmosphere through 3D modeling, texturing, lighting, and composition.

02

Forza Horizon 6 Trailer (fan made)

Recreating a Forza Horizon 6–inspired trailer using Blender 3D with a realistic visual approach. Set in a Japanese environment, this project focuses on atmosphere, lighting, environment details, and cinematic storytelling.

03

Kawal Putusan MK

A personal 3D project created in response to the public demonstrations surrounding the Constitutional Court’s decision. This render represents my contribution as a 3D artist—using visual storytelling to highlight that every voice, no matter how small, can have an impact. Through this piece, I aimed to capture the atmosphere of collective action and the importance of standing together.

04

Realistic Approach!

On the journey to "learning" how to create animation with a realistic approach through simple scene experiments, it's all about composition, light, and materials. Using tools and methods I've never worked with before, and also diving deeper into my previous workflows. It's been a lot of fun, and I've learned so much from this personal project. Yes it is! Enjoy! Gigantic shout out to @stache_obj for allowing me to recreate and be inspired by his work, go check out his page.
